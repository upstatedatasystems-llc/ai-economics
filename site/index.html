03 / EVIDENCE

What's measured and what's modeled?

Published rates

Consumer and API pricing, rented GPU instance rates and cited public benchmarks.

Scenario assumptions

Throughput, utilization, fleet scale, cost allocation, staffing and GPU-equivalent work.

Not publicly disclosed

Actual provider routing, per-user ad revenue, proprietary throughput and internal operating costs.

The infrastructure engine prices input and output as output-equivalent compute, divides by modeled annual utilized throughput, and adds allocated operations. In cloud mode, GPU rental already includes the lessor's facilities and capital expenses; owned mode calculates power, PUE, depreciation and maintenance separately. No training or research-and-development allocation is included. No claim is made about actual corporate margins or subsidies.
